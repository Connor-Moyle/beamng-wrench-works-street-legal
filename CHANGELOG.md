# Changelog: Wrench Works: Street Legal (the career)

## 0.4.0 (release 1 candidate, not yet released; needs Wrench Works 1.7.0)

What is new for players. Release 1 is the career on **West Coast USA**; Valo City (optional) works as before.

**West Coast USA**
- The career runs on West Coast USA. A new career asks which map (Valo City only when it is installed); the save remembers it.
- Places on the map: the junkyard, a dealer, a scrap yard, car meets, a hospital, a valet stand and car washes. The stock map's own dealerships and garages become car lots too: each sells from its own list, new or used.
- Street races on any map, made from the road network (sprints from the meets and the roads around), with the same rematch rule, trash talk and stakes as before.
- Laptops: walk up to a computer door (or stop a car at one) and the prompt "Use the laptop" opens your phone.
- Stock missions pay by type (evade, parking, time trials), and the tutorial uses a courier run where the map has no parts shops.
- What the map cannot host is hidden rather than half working: the ROC, circuit and venue races, the test-driver job, club circuit days and the swap meet are not offered on West Coast USA. Texts name the map you are playing on.

**Shops, lots and money**
- Dealer, junkyard and scrap yard open 08:00-17:00 (dealer 09:00-17:00) and say "Closed. Opens at HH:00" outside those hours; the garage's parts shop closes at 18:00.
- The junkyard always has a starter car at or under $3,500, and you start with $5,000.
- Money exploits closed: faults are only put right by a part in the slot at drive-out and pay at most 1.35x its cost; selling a cheap part back no longer clears them; a race you cannot cover is refused; the hot-car job pays less.
- Faults priced from an empty slot no longer count as fixed; the test-drive car stays until you are back on foot at the lot; a car bought or test driven leaves the lot on the nearest city lane.
- A teleport never repairs a car.

**Calmer and faster**
- Fewer per-frame costs from the markers, the lockdown and the race step.
- Jobs count a stop by distance along the ground; a diner's stop covers its whole row of pumps.

**Already in the career (not part of the release 1 promise)**: classifieds (Revline, the paper, Swap & Bid), clubs and leagues, rival crews and the turf war, the bank, garage upgrades, achievements, save slots (up to six careers), settings group, gamepad phone. They are in the zip and may be visible, but release 1 testing did not cover them; some need Valo City data.
